# ResumeOptimizer

> **Same Resume. Smarter Words.**

ResumeOptimizer is an AI-powered web application that helps job seekers tailor their resume for a specific job description while preserving the original formatting, structure, and design.

Unlike traditional resume builders that recreate your resume, ResumeOptimizer improves the content inside your existing Microsoft Word document so your resume still looks exactly the way you designed it.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📄 Upload Microsoft Word (.docx) resumes
- 🎯 Optimize resumes for specific job descriptions
- ✍️ AI-powered content rewriting
- 🧠 ATS-friendly wording suggestions
- 🎨 Preserve original formatting and layout
- 👀 Review and manually edit AI suggestions
- 💾 Download an optimized `.docx` file

---

## Why ResumeOptimizer?

Job seekers spend hours perfecting their resume design.

Most AI tools rebuild the resume and often change the formatting.

ResumeOptimizer follows a different philosophy:

> **Improve the words, not the design.**

Our goal is to help users create stronger resumes without losing the formatting they've carefully crafted.

## Development Philosophy

ResumeOptimizer is built around a few core principles:

- User experience comes first.
- Preserve the user's original resume.
- Keep the interface simple and intuitive.
- Let AI assist, not replace, the user.
- Build production-quality software with maintainable architecture.

---

## Vision

We believe every job application deserves a customized resume.

ResumeOptimizer aims to make resume optimization fast, reliable, and accessible while respecting the work users have already put into designing their resumes.
